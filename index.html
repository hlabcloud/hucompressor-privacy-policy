Privacy Policy for HUCompressor

Last updated: October 2026

HUCompressor ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

1. Local File Processing & Data Collection

HUCompressor is designed with privacy as a top priority. All photo and PDF compression processes are performed 100% locally on your device. We do NOT collect, upload, store, or share your personal files, documents, photos, or sensitive data to any external server or cloud.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies or unique device identifiers to serve ads based on user interactions. These third-party ad networks may collect non-personally identifiable information (such as device ID, IP address, and general location data) to provide personalized or contextual advertising.

You can learn more about how Google uses data when you use our app by visiting: Google Ads & Privacy Policies.

3. Permissions Required

To provide core compression functionality, HUCompressor requires access to your device's storage/media files. These permissions are strictly used to read the files you select for compression and save the compressed outputs back to your device storage.

4. Children's Privacy

HUCompressor does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
